D3TA1L3R audits your own public footprint. It queries only public, unauthenticated endpoints, obeys robots.txt, and reports sites it could not check as gaps rather than guessing. Scan files and breach reports contain personal data — delete them when you are done.

Password checks use k-anonymity: five characters of a SHA-1 hash leave this machine, never the password. Breach data is never downloaded or bundled — see docs/SCOPE.md.
